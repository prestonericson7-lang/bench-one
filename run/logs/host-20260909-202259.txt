 natal frame properties 
====================================================== 

===============================================================
the natal frame: 21 axes of 8192 bits
===============================================================

[1] identical on every node, with nothing transmitted
  two independent derivations agree bit for bit        yes

[2] axes are mutually unrelated, and LESS/MORE deliberately is not
      closest unrelated pair: AFTER/OTHER at 4000, furthest 4216, random expectation 4096
      LESS to MORE: 1548, which is the continuum's whole length
  no two independent axes drifted together             yes
  LESS and MORE are related but distinct               yes

[3] magnitude is smooth and monotonic -- the claim everything else rests on
      distance from value 0.0: 0.0=0 0.2=309 0.4=619 0.6=928 0.8=1238 1.0=1548
  further apart in value is further apart in space     yes
  the ends of the range ARE the LESS and MORE axes     yes
      0.3 to 0.4 is 155, 0.3 to 0.9 is 929
  a small change in value is a small change in space   yes

[4] nearby positions are nearby vectors
      one step 71, nine steps on one axis 697, far corner 1393
  distance in space tracks distance in the frame       yes

[5] a moment can be returned to the thing it was a moment of
      the thing three steps on sits 4086 from the thing itself
  unrotating recovers it exactly                       yes
  but a later moment is its own distinct thing         yes

[6] it can tell a fact about itself from a fact about the world
      200 of 200 classified correctly, mean margin 1042 bits
  self-knowledge lands in its own region               yes

  format   weight bytes   consumed        per MAC
  ------   ------------   ------------    -----------
  INT16         512 KB    30393.3 MB/s    15.93 G MAC/s
  INT8          256 KB    14926.7 MB/s    15.65 G MAC/s
  INT4          128 KB     1033.8 MB/s     2.17 G MAC/s

  INT4 halves the bytes but runs at 0.14x INT8's MAC rate.

====================================================== 
 does adding cores add bandwidth 
====================================================== 

===============================================================
does adding cores add memory bandwidth?
  8 logical cores, 64 MB streamed per thread, read only
===============================================================

  threads   aggregate GB/s   per thread   scaling vs 1 thread
  -------   --------------   ----------   -------------------
        1            10.23        10.23    1.00x of a perfect 1x
        2            19.91         9.96    1.95x of a perfect 2x
        4            28.01         7.00    2.74x of a perfect 4x
        8            29.63         3.70    2.90x of a perfect 8x

  one core alone reached 10.23 GB/s. All 8 together reached 29.63 GB/s,
  which is 2.90x, not 8x. The gap is the shared memory controller,
  and no amount of software removes it.

  measured per node in this project, streaming reads:
    Luckfox Pico Mini    0.930 GB/s      ESP32-S3  0.0576 GB/s
    Teensy 4.1 PSRAM    0.0328 GB/s

  boards needed to equal this whole machine's saturated 29.63 GB/s:
      32 Luckfoxes, and each one ADDS to the total rather than queueing
     514 ESP32-S3s

  a desktop's ceiling is fixed when it is bought. This one is bought in
  increments, and the increments do not contend.
