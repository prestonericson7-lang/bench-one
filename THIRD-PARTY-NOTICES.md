## Firmware images and bitstreams built here

`deploy/firmware/*.hex`, `deploy/firmware/*.merged.bin`, `bench-archive/**/*.hex` and
`firmware/bench-one/fpga/ddr3_ice40/bitstreams/*.bin` are built from this repository's own sources
(the build commands are beside them and in `deploy/run_all_tests.sh`). The microcontroller images
also contain the board vendors' Arduino cores (PJRC Teensyduino, Espressif arduino-esp32 / ESP-IDF),
which keep their own licences; the rtlsdr-pentest firmware is GPL-2.0-or-later as stated above.
